Conditions combine with and, or, not and brackets. Comparisons read the last closed bar; crosses_above and crosses_below read the last two, so they mean “crossed on the most recent bar” rather than “has ever crossed”.

A symbol without enough history to evaluate a clause is treated as not matching, never as probably matching — a screen that guesses hands you a list you can’t trust.
